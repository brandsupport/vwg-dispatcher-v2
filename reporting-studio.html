Reporting guardrails

      

        
First assignment ≠ every touch
Allocation share counts one initial assignment per unique ticket. Handover, reassignment and recovery activity stays separate.

        
Current workload is a snapshot
Open work is derived from the ticket-state export and is not historical allocation volume.

        
No invented timing metrics
Elapsed solve time is calculated only when explicit resolution and received timestamps are present and valid. Response time and active handling time remain unavailable without their own source events.

        
Uncertain data stays uncertain
Unknown inboxes, duplicate ticket rows, ambiguous initial assignments and unmapped states are counted as quality issues, not silently guessed.
